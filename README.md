# AI Readability

AI Readability measures how effectively AI systems can access, parse, and understand content.

## Key Factors

- Crawlability
- Structured Data
- Product Information
- Content Clarity
- Entity Coverage
- Machine Readable Content

## Why It Matters

If AI systems cannot reliably read a store, they cannot recommend it.

AI Readability is the foundation of AI Commerce Intelligence.
